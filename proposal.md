# Homework: Proposal
### The app. Which app are you cloning?
The app that I am choosing to clone is Setlog, a Korean mini-vlog mobile app. This app allows users to share short two-second clips with their friends for each hour of the day. 
### The fundamental problem. What is the core problem that this app solves for its users? Focus on the problem, not the feature list. For example, the problem a ride sharing app solves isn't "showing cars on a map," it's getting from point A to point B without owning a car or finding a taxi.
The core problem that Setlog solves is that it provides an easy, convenient way for users to keep up with their friends. It eliminates the need to manually edit a video log to send to their friends as it simplifies sending videos into a low-commitment, two-second clip. 
Additionally, since the app allows users to share a two-second clip every new hour of the day, it works against social media’s picture perfect narrative. I think the ultimate goal is to share an “unfiltered” version of daily life that keeps friendships authentic and real. 
### What you'll keep. What do you plan to do similarly to the original app, and why?
I will definitely keep the 24-hour clock with two-second vlogs. I think that feature is why Setlog is so popular and trending among gen z. It reminds me of the app BeReal, but instead of photos, it uses videos. In this way, it eliminates performative posts and overly curated content by users. As a result, it makes the social media platform more human as users are encouraged to post ordinary, everyday moments. These kind of posts humanize the users and make it feel more welcoming/relatable to viewers, which I believe heavily contributes to its popularity. 
Another aspect I’ll keep is the small room numbers. In Setlog, there’s a feature called “Rooms” which functions similar to the close friends feature on Instagram. It limits the audience/who can view your posts to a smaller number of people. This reduces the pressure and concern behind a user posting a video as since there is a smaller number of eyes, there is less judgement. Additionally, you can control who is in your room and what specific rooms your video goes to which further lowers the amount of thought that goes behind each video taken.  
Lastly, I love how each day’s hourly video logs are compiled into a single video that encapsulates your day. The calendar feature also lets you view past days and reminisce about the past and memories you made.
### What you'll change or remove. What do you plan to do differently, and why do you think your version will be better?
I think I’ll remove the ability to re-take hourly videos as it feels inauthentic. I think the whole purpose of the app is to be unpolished. The ability to retake a video feels like it adds to unnecessary overthinking on the poster’s part.
Also, I want to remove the ability to comment and react to videos. I just want users to focus on documenting their day without worrying about needing to show support to other users. My overall goal is to make documenting daily life feel like second nature for users.
I also want to add different recording orientations as it only accepts horizontal video recordings as of right now.
